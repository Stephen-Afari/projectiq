# Project Charter

## ERP Transformation Programme — Apex Manufacturing Ltd

**Document type:** Project Charter
**Version:** 1.0
**Date:** 2026-02-01
**Prepared by:** Priya Nair, Project Manager

## Purpose

Apex Manufacturing Ltd's legacy MRP system is over twelve years old, no
longer supported by its original vendor, and increasingly unable to
support the company's finance, procurement, and production planning
needs. This charter authorises the ERP Transformation Programme: the
replacement of the legacy MRP system with a unified ERP platform covering
Finance, Procurement, and Production Planning.

## Business Case

The legacy system requires manual reconciliation between finance and
production data on a weekly basis, costing an estimated 40 person-hours
per month across the finance and procurement teams. Vendor support for
the legacy platform ends in Q3 2027, after which no security patches will
be available. A unified ERP platform is expected to eliminate the manual
reconciliation burden, reduce month-end close time by approximately three
business days, and provide real-time visibility into procurement
commitments against budget.

## Scope

In scope:

- Finance module: general ledger, accounts payable, accounts receivable,
  budget management.
- Procurement module: purchase requisitions, purchase orders, vendor
  management, three-way matching.
- Production Planning module: material requirements planning, production
  scheduling, shop floor data collection.
- Migration of historical financial and production data from the legacy
  MRP system.
- Integration with the existing warehouse management system (no
  replacement of that system in this programme).

Out of scope:

- Human resources and payroll systems (remain on the current HR platform).
- Customer relationship management (a separate initiative, not yet
  scheduled).
- Replacement of the warehouse management system.

## Objectives and Success Criteria

1. Go-live of the Finance module by end of Q2 2026, followed by
   Procurement and Production Planning in a phased approach.
2. Reduce month-end financial close time from eight business days to five.
3. Eliminate manual reconciliation between finance and production
   systems.
4. Achieve user acceptance sign-off from Finance, Procurement, and
   Production Planning stakeholders before each module's go-live.
5. Complete the programme within the approved budget envelope, with any
   material overrun (greater than 10%) requiring a formal change request
   to the Steering Committee.

## High-Level Timeline

- **Programme kickoff:** 2026-02-10
- **Finance module go-live:** targeted end of Q2 2026 (subject to UAT
  sign-off)
- **Procurement module go-live:** Q3 2026
- **Production Planning module go-live:** Q4 2026
- **Programme close-out:** 2026-12-19

## Budget

The approved programme budget covers software licensing (120 named user
licences at initial scoping), Meridian Systems' implementation and
migration services, and internal resourcing. Any licensing quantity
change driven by downstream scoping (e.g. additional read-only seats for
shift supervisors) must be raised as a budget concern to the Steering
Committee before commitment.

## Key Risks Identified at Charter Stage

- Legacy MRP data quality is inconsistent, which may extend the
  migration timeline.
- Meridian Systems' resourcing capacity is a dependency the programme
  does not directly control.
- Quarter-end finance close windows constrain when Finance UAT can
  realistically run.
