the `risks` description — same substance, different categorization by
the model between runs, missed by a single-keyword match either way.
Left as-is rather than adjusted after the fact, per the golden set's
fixed-in-advance design; worth broadening this one keyword (e.g. to
match "productive" OR "delay") in a future golden-set revision, flagged
here for the PM rather than changed unilaterally.
